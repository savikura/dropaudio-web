Filmų įgarsinimas

Dokumentiniai filmai, trumpametražiai ir pilno metražo projektai. Profesionalūs balsai, dialogų miksas ir garso dizainas — viena studija.

Gauti pasiūlymą

Complete film audio — one studio

A film\'s soundtrack is not just a voice recording. It\'s ambient sounds, music, dialogue mix and effects that together create an emotional experience. DROPAUDIO is where you can get everything: from narrator casting to final mastering.

Sound design that makes you believe

A documentary viewer always senses when sound design was done carelessly — even if they can\'t name it. The action on screen sounds \'empty\', characters lose weight. We create a sound environment that makes the on-screen image real.

For narrator casting in documentary projects we apply different criteria than in advertising. It\'s not just voice quality that matters — it\'s a voice that matches the film\'s style, theme and target audience.

We can join at an early stage and advise on sound decisions during editing. Or receive finished video material and do everything from scratch to delivery.

We work with documentaries, short films and feature-length projects. Experience with TV channel, festival and private client films.
